This game is ultimately consent based. Your character sheet is meant to inform your RP, but ultimately it is up to you, your scene partners, and your Storyteller to decide the flow of play.

1. [[How to play|main:mt_how_to_play]]: An overview of expectation of conduct and examples.
2. [[Making a character|main:mt_making_a_character]]: This will pertain to the sheet itself, rather than going over who your character is.
3. [[Abilities and skills|main:mt_stats]]: every trait and what it adds.
4. [[Hit points, gear and advancement|main:mt_play_and_advancement]]: what you keep track of in play, and spending XP.
5. [[Encounters|main:mt_encounters]]: scenes played from copies of the sheets, with foes and turns.

Add [[Pantheons|main:mt_pantheons]] to the list.
